Report details

Include a day, underlying, instrument, expiry, price, client and side (Buy / Sell), for example: Today SX5E TRF Dec27 84 BNP Buy. Use a client name or alias registered in Lists & Clients; unmatched clients are saved as typed without an entity type.
Include total size, underlying, expiry, instrument, price / basis, buyer and seller. Use client names or aliases registered in Lists & Clients; unmatched clients are saved as typed without an entity type. A single seller inherits the total size; with multiple sellers, include sizes, with at most one seller left unsized to receive the remainder. Optional bracket references and a “futs” reference are retained.
For a roll, the buyer sells the near expiry and buys the far expiry.
